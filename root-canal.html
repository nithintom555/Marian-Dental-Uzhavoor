ROOT CANAL TREATMENT

What is Root Canal Treatment?

Root canal treatment is a dental procedure used to treat infection or damage affecting the inside of a tooth. The goal is to remove the infected tissue, clean the area and help preserve the natural tooth.

Why Might You Need a Root Canal?

A root canal may be recommended when the inner part of a tooth becomes infected or damaged due to decay, injury or other dental problems.

Benefits of Treatment

Treatment can help relieve dental discomfort, control infection and preserve the natural tooth.
